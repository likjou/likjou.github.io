How it works

Three steps, once it exists.

This is the flow Sheaf is meant to ship. It is not available to try, and it is not in the Chrome Web Store.

  1. Install the extension

    When Sheaf launches, add it to Chrome. There is nothing to install today.

  2. Filter your meetings

    Connect your own Read AI account through Read AI’s official API. Keep a date range, a keyword, or both.

  3. Download one ZIP

    Transcripts, summaries, and action items are packed in the formats you pick. The file is built in your browser.

Planned features

A Read AI bulk export, in one ZIP.

Sheaf will download Read AI transcripts, summaries, and action items for the meetings you filter. That is the bulk export Read AI does not offer.

  1. One ZIP

    The meetings you select go into a single archive instead of a separate download each time.

  2. Transcripts, summaries, and action items

    The three records a Read AI meeting already produces, kept together.

  3. Markdown, TXT, DOCX, CSV, and JSON

    Read a transcript in a notes app, open a summary in a word processor, or load action items into a spreadsheet.

  4. Date and keyword filters

    Limit the export to a range of dates, a keyword, or both. Take the whole library only if you want it.

  5. Runs locally in the browser

    The archive is assembled on your computer. Meeting data is not sent to our servers.

  6. Read AI’s official API

    Sheaf will use your own Read AI account through the official API. No meeting-by-meeting clicking.

Privacy

Your meetings stay in your browser.

Everything runs locally in the browser, uses Read AI’s official API, and no meeting data is sent to our servers.

  • Local by design

    The ZIP is assembled on your computer. Sheaf is not an upload bin for transcripts.

  • Your Read AI account

    The extension will call Read AI’s official API with your own login. The plan does not collect your password on a server of ours.

  • Waitlist answers only

    The form below emails your address and those questions. It has no field for meeting content.

FAQ

Before you join.

Is Sheaf available now?

Sheaf is launching soon and has not been built. It is not in the Chrome Web Store. This page cannot install it or sell it. The form only adds you to a waitlist.

Why not export from Read AI directly?

Read AI lets you export one meeting at a time. There is no download-all for transcripts, summaries, and action items. Sheaf is the bulk export this page is testing demand for.

Will my meeting data be sent to you?

No. Everything runs locally in the browser, uses Read AI’s official API, and no meeting data is sent to our servers. The waitlist form sends only your email and the answers on this page.

What will be inside the ZIP?

The meetings you filter, with transcripts, summaries, and action items, in Markdown, TXT, DOCX, CSV, and JSON.

Can I filter by date or keyword?

That is the plan: a date range, a keyword, or both, before anything is packed into the ZIP.

How much will it cost?

Free for 5 meetings. Waitlist members then pay an early-bird $19 one-time, instead of the planned $29 price. You cannot pay on this page. There is no subscription.

Are you affiliated with Read AI?

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Read AI. Sheaf is an independent project. You would use your own Read AI account. Read AI is named here only to describe what the extension exports.

What happens after I join?

You get an email when Sheaf is ready to try. Until then there is nothing to install. Joining does not charge you, and your address is not sent to Read AI.

Does Sheaf have users or reviews?

No. It has not launched, so there are no users and no reviews.
